Dual-Crop Multi-Region Trait Extraction

Methodology (Fish-Vista CVPR 2025):
Extracts diagnostic anatomical sub-regions: Cephalic ROI (anterior 35%) for snout angle, jaw dentition, and eye diameter ratio; and Caudal Peduncle ROI (posterior 25%) for peduncle depth, caudal spines (scalpel markers), and tail fin geometry.

Experimental Notice & Semantic Boundary:
The active vision backbones (BioCLIP, ResNet, DINOv2) were fine-tuned on whole-organism museum vouchers. When evaluated on severed fragments (such as an isolated tail fin without whole-body context), the network can encounter semantic ambiguity.

Implemented Mitigation (Adaptive Anchor-Preserving Gating):
The system protects the whole organism (Pglobal) as the primary invariant anchor. Sub-crops only boost confidence when they corroborate the anchor class (labeled Cephalic / Caudal Validated). Conflicting part predictions are safely gated out to prevent confidence dilution, maintaining high verified confidence.

Future Enhancement: End-to-end multi-task part fine-tuning on annotated anatomical keypoints directly in the feature latent space.
